## Platform
web
## Stack
User chose direct code-first construction. Buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript: classroom deployment without dependencies, accounts, or a compilation step.
## Users
Kazakh-speaking informatics pupils and their teachers; a pedagogical idea competition audience. Grades 5–8 confirmed by the user.
## Product Purpose
Develop algorithmic thinking through problem decomposition, executable sequences, branching, repetition, debugging and reflection. The user's title is «TamashaLike: жасанды интеллект арқылы оқушылардың алгоритмдік ойлау қабілетін дамыту».
## Operating Context
Classroom use, interactive whiteboard, laptop and mobile browsers. Competition presentation of the teaching method.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Working robot-grid interpreter, six exercises, diagnostics, reflective assessment, local progress and teacher view. AI support uses an explicit external ChatGPT handoff with a task-aware Socratic prompt. Local deterministic hints must never masquerade as model inference. No API credentials supplied. No invented pupil records or experimental impact claims. Local progress is per browser, not a school-wide backend.
## Brand Commitments
TamashaLike name and Kazakh language.
## Evidence on Hand
User supplied the title and teaching goal. No measured intervention data or real student data supplied.
## Product Principles
- Pupils predict before running and explain after running.
- Feedback points to a specific step.
- AI asks questions that preserve the pupil's authorship.
- Report observations, never invented effect sizes.

## Learning Flow Update
Mission drafts, predictions, paused execution, explanation and optional AI reflection persist per mission in the existing browser storage. A required explicit prediction/uncertainty choice precedes execution, followed by forecast/actual comparison. A/B diagnostics use different questions for corresponding skills and preserve retake history and variant labels. The teacher can score a four-criterion, 0–8 rubric and export actual observations or a blank observation template. A work label is an optional local report identifier, not an account or separate storage profile.
