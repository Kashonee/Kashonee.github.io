# Құқықтық мектеп кеңістігі: теориядан тәжірибеге
Kazakh educational website: explore a school in 360 degrees, first exterior, then lobby with 9 branches of law, then six scenario stations and congratulations. The user clarified: ordinary phone touch and laptop mouse, no VR headset. Preserve supplied definitions and scenarios while correcting inaccurate legal citations using official Kazakhstan sources. Success: student answers all eighteen questions with explanations and completes six stations.
## Platform
Responsive web; 360 degree drag, keyboard alternatives, touch. No account required. Local progress.
## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript. Original school geometry rendered into 360° panoramas; Canvas 2D displays them using self-hosted Three.js camera math. No WebGL or VR headset required.
